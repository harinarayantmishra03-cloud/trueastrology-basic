Bhinnashtakavarga (BAV) per planet and Sarvashtakavarga (SAV) per sign, from the classical Parashari tables. For a transiting planet, 4+ bindus is good; a sign with SAV 28+ is generally supportive.

Accuracy

The Sun and Moon use full Meeus series. The other planets use perturbed orbital elements. I compared every planet with the Swiss Ephemeris on 1, 6 and 12 January 2026, and all agreed to within about 1 arc-minute (the reference tables are rounded to the arc-minute). This is accurate for chart reading, but it is not arc-second precision. In KP, anything lying close to a sub-lord boundary is flagged in the Accuracy check. Rahu and Ketu use the mean node by default, and the true node can be chosen in Settings. The ayanamsa options are Lahiri, Raman and KP. Errors in birth time change the Lagna, the house cusps and the dasha, so please use an accurate birth time.

Privacy

Everything is calculated on your device. Birth details, saved profiles and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. The app has no accounts and no tracking, and it does not send your data anywhere.

🦚 Pancha Pakshi · Five Birds

Day planner

Week planner

Weekly strength grid

Pancha Pakshi is a traditional Tamil timing system. Your bird comes from the Moon's nakshatra and the paksha at birth. The day (sunrise to sunset) and the night (sunset to sunrise) are each split into five periods (yamas), and each yama into five sub-periods of 30, 36, 48, 18 and 12 minutes in a standard 144-minute yama (scaled to the real day length). Ruling is the strongest state and Dying the weakest. Published tables differ between schools, so treat this as traditional guidance. The quality ratings for sub-periods are an interpretive layer added here: friendly birds in strong states support you, and unfriendly birds in strong states press on you. Sunrise and sunset are calculated for your birth place.

🔮 Tarot · Readings with Dates and Times

📜 Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN)

BNN reads life events from how the planets link to each other in your chart (same sign, trine, opposite) and times them with the Jupiter and Saturn transits. It follows the method described in published guides on R.G. Rao's Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, and does not use the dasha. Besides the karaka planets, it also watches the lord of the house linked to each event.

🔱 Horary · Prashna Kundali

Ask one clear question and choose a number from 1 to 249. KP horary casts the planets for the moment of the question and fixes the Ascendant from your number. Then the CSL method gives the answer, the ruling planets confirm it and the dasha of that moment gives the timing. A Tajika Prashna reading of the same moment is added as a second opinion.
